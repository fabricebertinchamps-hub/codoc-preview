Select a regime for its procedures.

UAE

UAE onshore

Legal basis
Federal Decree-Law 51 of 2023 on Financial Restructuring and Bankruptcy, in force since 1 May 2024

Procedures

Preventive settlement (the debtor stays in control, with a three-to-six-month moratorium); restructuring under a court trustee; bankruptcy.

Worth knowing

Dedicated bankruptcy courts, court cram-down where creditors are no worse off than in liquidation, priority for new money, and a two-year look-back on director liability. It replaced the 2016 law used for Arabtec.
UAE

DIFC

Legal basis
Insolvency Law, DIFC Law 1 of 2019

Procedures

Rehabilitation with a 120-day moratorium and an insolvency practitioner as nominee; administration; winding up.

Worth knowing

Adopts the UNCITRAL Model Law on cross-border insolvency. Practitioners register with the DIFC Registrar.
UAE

ADGM

Legal basis
Insolvency Regulations 2022

Procedures

Administration; deed of company arrangement; scheme of arrangement; winding up.

Worth knowing

Modelled on UK law. NMC Health’s administration and exit is the landmark case. Matthew is on the ADGM Register of Insolvency Practitioners.
UAE

DMCC

Legal basis
DMCCA Company Regulations 2024

Procedures

Solvent, summary and insolvent voluntary winding up; court-ordered winding up.

Worth knowing

OW Bunker and Dynamic Oil Trading were the first DMCC insolvencies. Matthew acted as liquidator.
UAE

Dubai Decree 57

Legal basis
Decree 57 of 2009

Procedures

A bespoke tribunal for Dubai World and its subsidiaries, with English-style insolvency tools.

Worth knowing

Used for DryDocks World in 2012 and as the backstop for Dubai World in 2015, both with Matthew’s team as nominee.
GCC

Saudi Arabia

Legal basis
Bankruptcy Law, Royal Decree M/50 of 2018

Procedures

Protective settlement; financial reorganisation; liquidation; small-debtor versions; administrative liquidation.

Worth knowing

The Bankruptcy Commission licenses trustees. Cross-border rules followed in 2023.
GCC

Bahrain

Legal basis
Legislative Decree 22 of 2018

Procedures

Reorganisation in the style of Chapter 11; bankruptcy.

Worth knowing

An automatic stay that binds secured creditors.
GCC

Oman

Legal basis
Royal Decree 53 of 2019

Procedures

Restructuring through a Ministry of Commerce committee; preventive composition; bankruptcy.

Worth knowing

Plans of up to five years.
GCC

Kuwait

Legal basis
Law 71 of 2020, amended 2026

Procedures

Preventive settlement; restructuring; bankruptcy.

Worth knowing

The first specialised bankruptcy court in the region and a register of insolvency experts.
GCC

Qatar

Legal basis
Commercial Law 27 of 2006; QFC Insolvency Regulations

Procedures

Onshore bankruptcy under the Commercial Law; QFC regime for the financial centre.

Worth knowing

A new onshore bankruptcy law is in draft.

Earlier practice

United Kingdom, the Netherlands, Belgium and Australia, plus cross-border groups with operations in Oman, Qatar and Singapore.
