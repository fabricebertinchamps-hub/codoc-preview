Thirty years on every side of the restructuring table.

Company, lenders, government and court: Matthew has acted in each seat, in the UK, Benelux, Australia and, since 2013, the Gulf.

Matthew Wilde is a chartered accountant and insolvency practitioner who has spent his whole career in restructuring. He has worked on more than 220 cases, over 65 of them in the Middle East, and has led restructuring practices for PwC, AlixPartners and Teneo in the region.

He was Chief Restructuring Officer of NMC Health, then the UAE’s largest private healthcare operator, as its US$6.6bn debt position came to light, and of Al Jaber Group through its second restructuring. With PwC he acted as Decree 57 nominee on DryDocks World and Dubai World, advised the Saudi Ministry of Finance on Saudi Oger, and was liquidator in the first DMCC free-zone insolvencies. Shareholders named him liquidator of Arabtec Holding.

In 2026 he left Teneo and founded Wilde & Co as an independent practice. Gulf News has called him “one of the Middle East’s best-known restructuring specialists”. He speaks regularly at ADGM, INSOL and RILPA events on rescue finance, construction and real estate restructuring, and directors’ duties.

How he works
